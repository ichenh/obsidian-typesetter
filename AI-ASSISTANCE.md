# AI Assistance Policy

AI-assisted contributions are welcome when the contributor remains accountable for every submitted line.

Contributors must review generated code and prose, verify licenses and provenance, run the relevant checks, and disclose material AI assistance in the pull request. Do not submit secrets, private notes, personal data, or third-party confidential material to AI services.

AI output is not evidence that behavior is correct. Typography safety changes require focused automated tests and an honest description of any manual Obsidian validation that was or was not performed.
