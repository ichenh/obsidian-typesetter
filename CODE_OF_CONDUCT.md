# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, patient, and specific.
- Respect differing needs, languages, and technical experience.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Protect private information and avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized conduct.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the repository's security advisory channel. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate. Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
